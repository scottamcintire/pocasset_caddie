What isn't verified?

The original 3D view lacks a published camera transform and numeric height grid. The screenshot color is a relative ranking only; scanline flattening to an overhead frame is approximate. GPS polygons, pin coordinate fit, slope lines and putt direction require on-course or surveyed validation. This page is a design proof of concept, not competition-grade green-reading assistance.
